Capabilities

Our mission

"Make your dreams come true."

CNC Milling

3-, 4-, and 5-axis milling for complex geometries, pockets, and contoured surfaces held to tight tolerances.

CNC Turning

Precision turned parts, shafts, and threaded components with live tooling for combined milling and turning.

Prototyping

Fast, accurate one-off and low-volume parts so you can validate a design before committing to production.

Production Runs

Repeatable batch machining with documented process control, so part #1 and part #10,000 match.

Finishing

Deburring, anodizing, plating, and surface treatments coordinated in-house so parts arrive ready to use.

Inspection

Dimensional inspection and reporting on every job, with full traceability when your application demands it.
